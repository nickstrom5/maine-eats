What makes an icon

A place is here for a James Beard Foundation honor (an America's Classics award, a win, or a nomination or semifinalist nod) or for 40 or more years at its current address, checked against its own history page or local news. 10 of the 31 have James Beard honors and 22 have been at their address for 40 years or more. Honors are facts from the James Beard Foundation's own award records, not ratings.

The 31 on this list

The longest at the same address: Black Point Inn in Prouts Neck (Scarborough), there since 1878; Pentagöet Inn in Castine, there since 1894; The Goldenrod in York Beach (York), there since 1896; WaCo Diner in Eastport, there since 1924. Maine's oldest restaurants has the 22 places with 40 or more years at the same address.

How we made the list: we looked up each place in October 2026 and kept it only when a 2025 or 2026 source showed it in business at that address: its own site or menu, a dated post, Maine news reporting, or a town or chamber of commerce listing. A shack or stand that posts its opening and closing dates has them printed beside it; one we couldn't source this year was dropped. No review site fed into it, the order is by region and town, and no rating decides anything. Call ahead, since hours and menus shift. Our sources are on the about page.

Maine icons: questions

Which places here have James Beard honors?

Bagaduce Lunch in Penobscot (America's Classic), Eventide Oyster Co. in Portland (James Beard winner), Fore Street in Portland (James Beard winner), Nezinscot Farm in Turner (America's Classic), Norimoto Bakery in Portland (James Beard winner), Primo in Rockland (James Beard winner), Scales in Portland (James Beard winner), Standard Baking Co. in Portland (James Beard winner), The Quarry in Monson (James Beard winner) and Zu Bakery in Portland (James Beard winner). James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation.

Is the Icons order a ranking?

No. In the web search and the app, the Icons guide puts the most honored and longest-running places first, using the honors and years above. It isn't a rating of the food, and we never show it as a score.

Why isn't a famous place here?

We add a place only after checking it against a 2025 or 2026 source and confirming its honor or its year at the same address. A place that moved counts from its move. Tell us about one at nick@eatsranked.com.
