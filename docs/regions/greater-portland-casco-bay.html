About this region

The Greater Portland & Casco Bay region is Cumberland County. Of the 1,120 places to eat and drink the app knows here, the most are in Portland, South Portland and Brunswick. Our hand-checked lists have 44 places here; the towns with the most are Portland (23), South Portland (6) and Scarborough (4). The most common kinds of place are Bar & Pub (211), Coffee & Café (141) and Bakery & Sweets (117). The longest at the same address is Black Point Inn in Prouts Neck (Scarborough), there since 1878.

How we draw it: the Maine Office of Tourism's Greater Portland & Casco Bay region, on county lines. Counties: Cumberland. A place is in it when its town is in one of those counties. Town pages here: Portland, South Portland, Brunswick, Scarborough, Westbrook, Cape Elizabeth, Freeport.
