About this region

The Lakes & Mountains region is Androscoggin, Franklin and Oxford counties. Of the 489 places to eat and drink the app knows here, the most are in Lewiston, Auburn and Farmington. Our hand-checked lists have 20 places here; the towns with the most are Lewiston (5), Auburn (3) and Turner (2). The most common kinds of place are Bar & Pub (93), Coffee & Café (68) and Bakery & Sweets (56). The longest at the same address is Labadie's Bakery in Lewiston, there since 1925.

How we draw it: the Maine Office of Tourism's Lakes & Mountains region, on county lines. Counties: Androscoggin, Franklin, Oxford. A place is in it when its town is in one of those counties. Town pages here: Lewiston, Auburn.

Red snappers & Italians in the Lakes & Mountains region

2 places, by town. The statewide guide has 11.

  1. Sam's Italian Foods

    40 Millett Dr, Auburn

    Maine Italians Known for: Maine Italian sandwich; pizza

    Lewiston-Auburn's own Maine Italian sandwich chain (since 1939, now employee-owned), with shops in Auburn, Lewiston, Lisbon Falls, Augusta, Dixfield and Brunswick.

    samsitalian.com

  2. Simones' Hot Dog Stand

    99 Chestnut St, Lewiston

    Red snappers Known for: red snapper hot dogs on steamed buns; chili dogs

    Downtown Lewiston hot dog stand run by the Simones family for four generations, known for red snappers ('Lewiston lobsters') on steamed buns; open weekdays.

Whoopie pies & donuts in the Lakes & Mountains region

3 places, by town. The statewide guide has 16.

  1. Labadie's Bakery

    161 Lincoln St, Lewiston

    Whoopie pies Known for: whoopie pies · At this address since 1925

    Century-old bakery in Lewiston's old Little Canada neighborhood, built on whoopie pies; retail counter open weekday mornings and ships nationwide.

  2. Katherine's Bakery & Cafe

    248 Main St, Norway

    Whoopie pies Known for: whoopie pies; doughnuts; breakfast and lunch sandwiches

    Downtown Norway bakery-cafe whose owner is known for whoopie pies (it once baked 3,200 for a single order); judged best flavored whoopie pie at the 2025 Maine Whoopie Pie Festival and third-best traditional in 2026.

  3. Valley View Orchard Pies

    15 Madison Ave, Oxford

    Whoopie piesBlueberry pie Known for: whoopie pies; Maine wild blueberry pie; fruit and cream pies

    Family-owned pie bakery that welcomes visitors during weekday business hours; its whoopie pie won best traditional at the 2026 Maine Whoopie Pie Festival, and Maine wild blueberry is its most popular pie.

Franco-American food in the Lakes & Mountains region

1 place, by town. It's the only one in the statewide guide so far.

  1. Grant's Bakery

    525 Sabattus St, Lewiston

    Franco-American Known for: pork pie (tourtière); salmon pie; baked beans and brown bread

    Family bakery since 1956 that bakes hot pork pies (tourtière) on Wednesdays and salmon pie on Fridays; its tourtière was in the Sun Journal's 2015 local tourtière tasting.
