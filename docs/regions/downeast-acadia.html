About this region

The Downeast & Acadia region is Hancock and Washington counties. Of the 408 places to eat and drink the app knows here, the most are in Bar Harbor, Ellsworth and Southwest Harbor. Our hand-checked lists have 23 places here; the towns with the most are Mount Desert (3), Bar Harbor (2) and Eastport (2). The most common kinds of place are Bar & Pub (87), Seafood (68) and Coffee & Café (56). The longest at the same address is Pentagöet Inn in Castine, there since 1894.

How we draw it: the Maine Office of Tourism's Downeast & Acadia region, on county lines. Counties: Hancock, Washington. A place is in it when its town is in one of those counties. Town pages here: Bar Harbor, Ellsworth, Mount Desert.
