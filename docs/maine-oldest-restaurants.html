Maine's oldest restaurants

22 Maine places that have fed people at the same address for 40 years or more, oldest first: 3 from before 1900, 8 from 1900 to 1949, 8 from 1950 to 1979 and 3 from 1980 on. The first, Black Point Inn in Prouts Neck (Scarborough), has been there since 1878.

What the year means

The year is when the place started serving at the spot where it stands today, taken from its own history page or from Maine news coverage. Moving resets the count, so a business founded long ago can show a later year; a place that changed its name but kept its spot can keep the earlier one. The year tells you how long, not how good. Only places we checked by hand carry a year, so many of the app's 4,198 other restaurants may be just as old without showing it here.

How we made the list: we looked up each place in October 2026 and kept it only when a 2025 or 2026 source showed it in business at that address: its own site or menu, a dated post, Maine news reporting, or a town or chamber of commerce listing. A shack or stand that posts its opening and closing dates has them printed beside it; one we couldn't source this year was dropped. No review site fed into it, the order is by years at the same address, and no rating decides anything. Call ahead, since hours and menus shift. Our sources are on the about page.

This is the Oldest Places list in the app and in the web search. What each one is known for is in its entry below and in the lobster shacks & rolls, clam shacks, red snappers & Italians, whoopie pies & donuts, diners, oyster bars and dairy bars guides.
