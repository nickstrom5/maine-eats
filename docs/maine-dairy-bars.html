Soft serve, hard serve and a frappe

A Maine dairy bar is a roadside ice cream stand, usually a walk-up window with soft serve, hard ice cream and frappes (in New England, a frappe is a milkshake made with ice cream).

The 54 on this list

31 are seasonal and 20 post their dates; 11 of those open in March or April. 17 make their own ice cream and 7 scoop Gifford's, the Skowhegan ice cream maker, as their notes say. The longest at the same address: The Goldenrod in York Beach (York), there since 1896; The Dairy Port in Bucksport, there since 1954; Jordan's Snack Bar in Ellsworth, there since 1981. Maine's oldest restaurants has the 22 places with 40 or more years at the same address.

How we made the list: we looked up each place in October 2026 and kept it only when a 2025 or 2026 source showed it in business at that address: its own site or menu, a dated post, Maine news reporting, or a town or chamber of commerce listing. A shack or stand that posts its opening and closing dates has them printed beside it; one we couldn't source this year was dropped. No review site fed into it, the order is by region and town, and no rating decides anything. Call ahead, since hours and menus shift. Our sources are on the about page.

In the Maine Eats app the same places come nearest-first from wherever your phone is, and each opens Apple's own map card for opening times, pictures and the route.

Dairy bars: questions

When do Maine dairy bars open?

Many open in early spring and close in the fall. Of the 54 here, 31 are seasonal; 20 post their dates, and 11 of those open in March or April. The season is listed with each place, and a stand that's closed for the winter hasn't closed for good.

What's a frappe?

In New England, a frappe is a milkshake made with ice cream. Ask for a milkshake and you may get milk and syrup without any ice cream.

Who makes the ice cream?

17 of the 54 make their own, as their notes say, and 7 scoop Gifford's, made in Skowhegan. The “Known for” line with each place says what it serves.
