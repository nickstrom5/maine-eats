Portland at a glance

The app's directory has 442 places to eat and drink in Portland, including 4 in Peaks Island. The most common kinds are Bar & Pub (113), Coffee & Café (53) and Bakery & Sweets (37). 265 are matched to a Maine liquor license on the state's list. 31 are locations of a chain with five or more in Maine. 23 are on our hand-checked lists, 6 of them with James Beard honors; the longest at the same address is Becky's Diner, there since 1991. Portland is in Cumberland County, in the Greater Portland & Casco Bay region.
