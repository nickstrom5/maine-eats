On our lists in Boothbay Harbor

The places in Boothbay Harbor on our hand-checked lists, most featured first: James Beard honorees and long-running places lead. That order isn't a rating.

  1. Dunton's Doghouse

    40 Sea St, Boothbay Harbor

    Red snappers Known for: red hot dogs (W.A. Bean); Riley Dog with cheese and bacon; hot dog with coleslaw

    Small hot dog stand at the edge of downtown Boothbay Harbor serving natural-casing dogs and W.A. Bean red hot dogs on griddled, buttered New England-style buns.

  2. Mine Oyster

    16 Wharf St (Pier 1), Boothbay Harbor

    Oyster bar Known for: raw oysters

    Waterfront raw bar and night club on Pier 1 run by chef Ralph Smith; it hosts the annual Pemaquid Oyster Festival with oysters from about 10 Damariscotta River farms (2025 and 2026).

    2 Elm St, Damariscotta · 13 mi from Boothbay Harbor
