The state treat and the state dessert

The whoopie pie, two rounds of soft chocolate cake around a sweet white filling, is Maine's official state treat, and wild blueberry pie is the official state dessert; the Legislature named both in 2011. A potato donut is made with mashed potato in the dough, which keeps it moist.

Whoopie pies & donuts: questions

Is the whoopie pie really Maine's state treat?

Yes. In 2011 the Maine Legislature named the whoopie pie the official state treat and wild blueberry pie the official state dessert. 10 places on this list are known for whoopie pies.

What is a potato donut?

A doughnut made with mashed potato in the dough, which keeps it moist and a little dense. 4 places on this list make them.

Where can I get blueberry pie?

On this list: Moody's Diner in Waldoboro, Fox's Lobster House in York Beach (York), Dysart's Restaurant & Truck Stop in Hermon and Valley View Orchard Pies in Oxford. Wild blueberries ripen in August, and many places bake with frozen ones the rest of the year.

Which bakery or diner here has been at its address the longest?

Of the 5 places on this list with a checked year at the same address, the longest-running is Labadie's Bakery in Lewiston, there since 1925. Places without a checked year may be older, so this answers for our list, not for the whole state. Maine's oldest restaurants has the 22 places with 40 or more years at the same address.
