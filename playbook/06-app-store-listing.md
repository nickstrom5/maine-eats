## Description
```
Maine's restaurants, from the wharf-side lobster pounds to the diners on Route 1.

Maine Eats is a free guide to eating in Maine: lobster shacks and pounds, clam shacks, red snapper hot dogs and Maine Italians, whoopie pies and potato donuts, diners, oyster bars and dairy bars we checked open by hand, plus 4,225 restaurants, cafés, bars and bakeries in 319 Maine towns. No account, no ads.

MAINE GUIDES
• Lobster Shacks & Rolls: pounds, shacks and wharves from Kittery to Eastport, with each one's season where it posts it.
• Clam Shacks: whole-belly fried clams, chowder and clam cakes.
• Red Snappers & Italians: the bright-red natural-casing hot dog and the Maine Italian sandwich.
• Whoopie Pies & Donuts: whoopie pies, potato donuts and wild blueberry pie.
• Diners, Oyster Bars and Dairy Bars: long-running diners, Damariscotta River and Casco Bay oysters, and the summer ice cream stands.
• Maine Icons: James Beard honorees, America's Classics and places with 40 or more years at their address.
• Oldest Places: years at the same address, oldest first.

FIND IT FAST
• Sort by distance, or browse the map by lobster, clam shacks, sweets or dairy bars.
• Search by name, town, village or street: "lobster roll portland", "cape neddick", "route 1".
• Filter by town or cuisine, hide chains.
• Save places for your next trip.
• Find hand-checked places from iPhone search (Spotlight).

LIVE DETAILS FROM APPLE MAPS
Tap "Ratings, hours & photos" on a place to open Apple Maps' own place card, with current hours, photos, ratings and directions.

HOW THE LISTS ARE BUILT
Every place in the Maine guides was checked by hand in October 2026 against a 2025 or 2026 source: its own website or menu, local news, or a town or chamber listing. Many coastal places are seasonal; a shack closed for the winter isn't closed. The rest of the restaurants come from Overture Maps open data and the State of Maine's liquor-license list; map listings are kept only at high confidence, measured against that list. Maine's official inspection result is Pass or Fail; where the City of Lewiston posts it, a place shows its latest result with the date. Website links are checked too. Nothing is ranked by star ratings.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no ads. Your location, if you share it, only sorts lists on your device. Saved places stay on your device.

Places open and close, and many shacks are seasonal, so check before you go. Spot a mistake? Email work-with-nick@gmail.com.

An independent app, not affiliated with any restaurant, brand or government agency, or with the James Beard Foundation. James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation. Place data from Overture Maps Foundation (CDLA Permissive 2.0) and others; sources and licenses are on the About tab.
```
