# Leads: York County ("The Maine Beaches")

Places that probably belong in a guide but could not be verified under the rules (checked 2026-10-08).

- **Flo's Hot Dogs**, 1359 Route 1, Cape Neddick (York): OPEN per a dated source (Press Herald 2026-05-04, https://www.pressherald.com/2026/05/04/13-maine-hot-dog-spots-to-visit-this-summer/, "open Thursday through Sunday"; John and Gail Stacy and their daughter run it), but NO kind fits: no source says it serves red snappers. Press Herald says the dogs are boiled on steamed buns; Yankee (2016, https://newengland.com/food/flos-hot-dogs-local-flavor/) says they are salty all-beef dogs from Old Neighborhood in Lynn, Mass. The README's "red snapper" example for Flo's looks wrong. Its own site (floshotdogs.com) still shows a 2024 closure notice. Florence Stacy bought an existing stand in 1959 (the relish came from original owner Robert Johnson), so 1959 is not a founding year. Needs a call on whether a hot-dog kind is wanted.
- **Bob's Clam Hut**, 315 US Route 1, Kittery 03904: own site (bobsclamhut.com, "since 1956", © 2026, live online ordering) has no dated season, event or news, and no 2025-26 dated story turned up. The same group owns Mabel's Lobster Claw, Robert's Maine Grill and Harbor Burger Shack (own site's "Other Properties" list).
- **Chauncey Creek Lobster Pier**, 16 Chauncey Creek Rd, Kittery Point (Kittery): own site (chaunceycreek.com) gives hours "Mother's Day thru Labor Day" and "Post-Labor Day thru Columbus Day" but no year. No 2025-26 source found. Founding is disputed: the home page says "established in 1948", its About page says great-uncle Herb Witham built the pier in the late 1930s, and Down East (published 2023) calls it "75-year-old".
- **Ogunquit Lobster Pound**, 504 Main St, Ogunquit 03907: own site says "Established in 1931" and "Now in our 93rd year" (that would be 2023 or 2024, so stale). History page: Hamilton and Varney families started it in 1931, ran it 10 years, closed two years in WWII, and William Hancock reopened it in 1944. Only third-party 2025-26 mentions (a hotel gift-certificate offer, a tour package), and an aggregator marks it "temporarily closed". Would qualify as `oldest` if open.
- **Mabel's Lobster Claw**, 124 Ocean Ave, Kennebunkport 04046: own site (mabelslobster.com) still says "Reopening on April 22nd for the 2024 season"; newest menu PDF is from September 2023. Same ownership group as Bob's Clam Hut.
- **Cape Neddick Lobster Pound**, Cape Neddick (York): own site (capeneddicklobsterpound.com) says "Open since 1959" and open year-round, but nothing dated 2025-26. visitmaine.com listing is behind a bot check.
- **The Lobster Shack**, Perkins Cove, Ogunquit (lobster-shack.com): site says the building became Maxwell Perkins Lobster Pound in 1947 and the Evans family has run "the Shack" since 1986; site last updated 2020. No 2025-26 source. Would be an `oldest` candidate (1947) if verified.
- **Warren's Lobster House**, 9-13 Water St, Kittery: per Press Herald (2025-06-24) it ran takeout-only until the start of 2025, then the property sold at auction for $2.25 million to Brian Thibeault (ZJBV Properties), who said he wants to keep it a working waterfront and keep the restaurant going; Mainebiz said the restaurant had closed. No source shows it reopened, and none says it closed for good. Lobster service on the site dates to 1940.
- **Ted's Fried Clams**, Emery Mills Rd (Route 109), Shapleigh: opened 1950, closed 2023; new owners planned to relaunch May 1, 2026 (Portland Food Map 2026-02-28). No independent source confirms it reopened; its own site (tedsfriedclams.com) disallows ClaudeBot in robots.txt, so it cannot be cited.
- **Rapid Ray's**, 189 Main St, Saco 04072: own site (rapidrays.net, © 2026) says serving since 1953 (Ray Camire started from a bread truck; a 2024 Press Herald snippet says the current building opened in 1986). Menu has steamed hot dogs, clam cakes, lobster roll and new poutine, but no red snappers and no dated 2025-26 source; no kind clearly fits.
- **George's Sandwich Shop**, 37 Franklin St, Biddeford 04005: Maine-style Italian sandwiches "since 1948" (own site, georgessandwichshop.com; per search results the Ladakakos family has been at this address since 1998). Own site undated; no 2025-26 source found. Good `italian sandwich` candidate.
- **Amato's (York County branches)**: search results show a Biddeford Amato's, but amatos.com's location list loads by script and could not be read; not verified.
- **Shain's of Maine**, 1491 Main St, Sanford 04073: homemade ice cream and family restaurant (ice cream shop from 1980, restaurant under one roof by 1984, per own site); own site undated.
- **Mike's Clam Shack**, 1150 Post Rd, Wells: own site only shows "Copyright 2021-2026"; started 1991 in Moody, moved to this site in the early 2000s.
- **Pool Lobster (F.O. Goldthwaite's)**, 3 Lester B. Orcutt Blvd, Biddeford Pool (Biddeford) 04006: general store, restaurant and lobster pound; site says F.O. Goldthwaite founded it in 1902 as a fish market; nothing dated 2025-26.
- **Billy's Chowder House**, Wells: own site (billyschowderhouse.com) answers with a bot check; not fetched.
- **Langsford Road Lobster & Fish House**, Cape Porpoise (Kennebunkport): seen only in an undated travel guide; not checked.
- **Congdon's Doughnuts**, Wells (congdons.com): open (own site updated 2026; a new generation took over management in 2025 per Portland Food Map), at this spot since 1955 (the 1945 Kennebunk restaurant was a different address). Not added: its doughnuts are not described as potato doughnuts and no other kind fits.
- **Reilly's Bakery**, Biddeford: said to make whoopie pies (an undated blog); its own site is unreachable; no 2025-26 source.
- **Auntie's House Bake House**, Kennebunk: whoopie pies and whoopie balls per an undated blog; site unreachable.
- **Salty Spirits**, 76 Wallingford Square, Kittery: opened Dec. 18, 2025 with raw-bar items (Press Herald 2025-12-31); it's a small wine and cocktail bar, so `oyster bar` didn't fit well.
- **The Flounder**, York Beach (Sand Dollar Bar & Grille building): counter cafe with a raw bar, lobster rolls and chowder, opened spring 2026 (Portland Food Map 2026-05-28); Instagram-only, no address.
- **West Street Market**, Biddeford: sold tourtiere and cretons (Press Herald 2017); no 2025-26 source. Franco-American food in Biddeford-Saco turned up nothing current.
- **Sacred Profane**, 50 Washington St, Biddeford: brewery whose menu has included cretons and poutine (2022); no current menu checked.
