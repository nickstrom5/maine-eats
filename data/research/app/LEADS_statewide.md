# Statewide leads (Franco-American, Italians, red snappers, potato doughnuts, whoopie pies, oldest)

Places that probably belong in a guide but couldn't be verified under the rules (checked 2026-10-09). One line each with why.
"BABLO" = the state liquor-license file current as of 10/1/2026 (https://www.maine.gov/dafs/bablo/liquor-licensing/license-data),
already in `data/me/official.pkl`; an active license is a dated official record, but it doesn't say what's on the menu.

## Franco-American
- **Lakeview Restaurant**, 9 Lakeview Dr, St. Agatha 04772: open (BDN Jan 2025; BABLO Class A restaurant/lounge license active to 2027-06). Poutine and ployes appear only in Visit Aroostook's 2026 "Best of Aroostook" readers' poll; its Visit Aroostook listing describes steaks, seafood, lobster rolls and prime rib; own site lakeviewrestaurant.biz returns 526.
- **The Swamp Buck Restaurant & Lounge**, 250 W Main St, Fort Kent 04743: BABLO license active; swampbuck.com renders no text (script-only), and poutine is only in the readers' poll. No dish source.
- **Rock's Family Diner**, Fort Kent: already in inland.json (diner, whoopie pie); its site mentions no ployes, poutine or other Acadian dishes, so not tagged franco american.
- **Inn of Acadia**, 384 St. Thomas St, Madawaska: dining page lists dinner Tue–Sat only, nothing Acadian on it.
- **Two Rivers Lunch**, Allagash: no site found (tworiverslunch.com unreachable); poutine only in the readers' poll.
- **T&D Eatery**, Madawaska: Acadian food truck (chicken stew with ployes, pâté chinois, pot en pot; The County 2026-07-14, https://thecounty.me/2026/07/14/news/aroostook-food-truck-offering-acadian-cuisine-and-more-on-the-northern-border/); still no fixed address.
- **The Cooperage**, Bridgewater (Route 1): new owners opened it Memorial Day 2026 with poutine specials and a planned poutine section (The County 2026-07-21, https://thecounty.me/2026/07/21/news/quirky-northern-maine-takeout-brings-urban-fare-to-the-county/); seasonal takeout, poutine is fusion (Thai chili, fajita), no street number; weak fit for franco american.
- **Italian Deli & Bakery**, 225 Bartlett St, Lewiston: open (Press Herald doughnut guide, 2025-05-08); its tourtière is only documented as "the Italian Bakery" in the Sun Journal's 2015 tasting. Needs a current source for meat pies.
- **E.W. Mailhot Sausage Co.**, 258 Bartlett St, Lewiston: own site (mailhotsausage.com) says Franco-American products since 1910, but it reads as a producer selling through grocers; no retail counter or hours found.
- **Fernleaf Bakery and Coffee House**, 20 Free St, Saco: Press Herald (2026-04-04) mentions "meat-filled hand pies"; not described as tourtière.
- **Bisson & Sons Meat Market**, Topsham: sells cretons and tourtière at the holidays per a 2012 NPR piece only; no site found.
- **Dolly's Restaurant** is in statewide.json on the strength of its active BABLO license (renewed, expires 2027-09-07) plus the Visit Aroostook listing and Down East (2020). Down East answered our article fetch with 403, so only its title/summary ("cretons, poutine, and ployes") was read, via the site's own API. If a BABLO license isn't acceptable evidence, move Dolly's back here.

## Maine Italian sandwiches
- **Corsetti's**, Westbrook: open in Feb 2025 (Press Herald: Eli Small has owned it since 2018 and kept the Italians); its site corsettis.com answers with a bot check and no usable source gives a street address.
- **Quality Shop**, 473 Stevens Ave, Portland: Portland Food Map directory has the address; only "named by readers on Facebook" in the Press Herald (2025), no dated source showing it open.
- **Severino's**, Westbrook: same Facebook-thread mention only; no address.
- **Gerard's Pizza**, Gardiner: 60th anniversary in 2025 with a $2 large Italian sandwich special (Portland Food Map 2025-01-20); Italians not shown to be its signature; address not checked.
- **Mancini's Italian Deli**, 5 Park St, Lewiston: passed a city inspection 2025-01-30 (lewistonmaine.gov/621); Maine Italians not confirmed.
- **Monte's Fine Foods**, Portland: Wikipedia says it added the first vegan Italian in 2019; no 2025-26 source found.
- **Sam's Italian Foods branches**: samsitalian.com/alllocations lists shops at 229 Center St (Auburn), 137 Civic Center Dr (Augusta), 4 Stephen Dr (Brunswick), 28 Main St (Dixfield), 902 Lisbon St and 675 Main St (Lewiston) and more; only the Auburn main shop is in inland.json. They can be added as branch records if the guide wants chain branches.
- **Amato's branches**: about half of Amato's 40-plus shops are in Maine (Wikipedia); amatos.com's location list loads by script. Same call as Sam's.
- **Goodfella's**, Western Ave, Augusta: only reader comments on a Yankee page; not verified.

## Red snappers
- **QP Burger**, Martinsville (St. George): takeout opened 2025 serving W.A. Bean red snappers (Portland Food Map 2025-06-29 and 2025-08-13, citing The Villager); Instagram-only, no street address; The Villager (midcoastvillager.com) blocks ClaudeBot.
- **Allagash Bungalow**, Allagash Brewing, 50 Industrial Way, Portland: Bite Into Maine runs the kitchen, menu includes "red hots" (Portland Food Map 2025-08-02); not clearly red snappers or a signature.
- **Lonnie Dogs**, Gray, and **Cormier's Dog House**, Windham: see LEADS_portland.md (cart with no address; red snapper not the signature).
- No other stand with red snappers as a signature turned up in Press Herald, BDN, Portland Food Map or Maine Atlas coverage for 2025-26.

## Potato doughnuts
- **Donut Grove**, 153 Park St Suite B, Orono: vegan doughnut shop with a "country potato with cinnamon sugar" flavor (Press Herald 2025-05-08); own site donutgrove.com unreachable; not described as a potato-doughnut shop.
- **The Holy Donut, Arundel** (1197 Portland Rd): listed with "hours TBD" in the Press Herald's May 2025 guide, but it is not among the three locations on theholydonut.com now (Oct 2026). Unconfirmed whether it ever opened.
- **Sadie's Bakery**, Water St, Houlton: famous molasses doughnuts (Wednesdays); open in July 2026 (The County 2026-07-07); Visit Aroostook says Aroostook molasses doughnuts were traditionally made with potatoes, but no source says Sadie's are. Not "oldest" either: it began in 1948 on Military St and moved twice (The County 2016).

## Whoopie pies
- **Honey and Lace** bakery, Sedgwick: judged best traditional whoopie pie at the 2025 festival (Piscataquis Observer 2025-06-16); honeyandlace.com doesn't load; no address.
- **Northport Campground Cafe and Bakery**, Northport: 2nd traditional and 3rd flavored at the 2026 festival; its domain now serves a gambling spam page; no address.
- **Elaine's Basket Cafe**, Milo; **Kindred Farms Market and Bakery**, Casco; **Shady Tree Farm**, Livermore Falls; **Backyard Bakery**, Dixmont; **Through the Tulips Bakery**, Milo: 2025-26 festival prize winners; no sites or addresses verified.
- **Wicked Whoopies**: wickedwhoopies.com is a live mail-order shop (Sept 2026 shipping notice) but shows no walk-in store.
- **Cranberry Island Kitchen**, Portland: its domain now serves an unrelated spam site; status unknown.
- **Brill's Bakery**, 27 Summer St, Wiscasset: opened Sept 2026 with whoopie pies among many items (Portland Food Map 2026-09-02); too new to call known for them.
- **Sweets & Co.**, 294 Main St, Saco: gluten-free bakery that "also offers whoopie pies" (Press Herald 2026-04-04); not known for them.

## Oldest (at the same address since 1950 or earlier)
- **Ogunquit Lobster Pound**, 504 Main St, Ogunquit: BABLO Class A license active to 2027-07-13, but its own site is stale ("93rd year") and an aggregator shows "temporarily closed"; see LEADS_beaches.md for its 1931/1944 history.
- **Poland Spring Resort**, Robbins Way, Poland: open year-round dining for the first time in 50 years (Sun Journal 2025-12-08); the Rickers began running an inn on the property in 1794 (own history page), but today's dining rooms are newer: the Maine Inn was built in 1962 and Mel's Hilltop opened after the 1975 Poland Spring House fire. Not clearly "same address since 1950."
- **Provender Kitchen + Bar**, 112 Main St, Ellsworth: BDN (2026-02-16) calls it Ellsworth's oldest continuously operating restaurant building, but gives no year.
- **The Lucerne Inn**, 2517 Main Rd, Dedham: part of the 1925 Lucerne-in-Maine development (BDN 2025-07-01); the year the inn began serving meals isn't stated; site shows only "© 2026".
- **Reilly's Bakery**, 232 Main St, Biddeford: open in 2026 (Press Herald 2026-02-16 and 2026-04-04: "more than 110 years old," four generations); the year it reached 232 Main St isn't stated and reillysbakery.com is unreachable.
- **George's Sandwich Shop**, Biddeford: in statewide.json as an Italian shop; "since 1948," but the year it reached 37 Franklin St isn't stated.
- **Nonantum Resort** (Kennebunkport, "first opened in the late 1800s"), **Union Bluff Hotel** (York Beach, "since 1868"), **Spruce Point Inn** (Boothbay Harbor), **York Harbor Inn**: their sites show only "© 2026" or undated offers, and none gives a year meals began at today's building.
- **The Village Inn of Belgrade Lakes**, 157 Main St, Belgrade: BABLO license active but expiring 2026-10-26; villageinnmaine.com renders no text. Down East (2023) dated it to about 1921 as the Locust House.
- **Helen's Restaurant**, 111 Main St, Machias (blueberry pie lead in LEADS_downeast.md): BABLO license active to 2027-05-12, the dated official record that lead was missing. Not "oldest" (moved to this site in 1983).
- **Day's Crabmeat & Lobster**, Yarmouth: BABLO license active, but the license lists 126 Route 1, not 1269 US Route 1; worth a look.

- Dolly's Restaurant, 17 US Route 1, Frenchville (ployes, poutine, cretons): moved back from statewide.json on 2026-10-09. Its only 2026 evidence is an active BABLO liquor license; the guides need a 2025-26 source showing it open (its own site/menu, dated news, or a dated town/chamber listing). Nick may decide to accept a 2025-26 license term as that evidence.
