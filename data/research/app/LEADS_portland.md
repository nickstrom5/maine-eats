# Leads: Cumberland County (Greater Portland & Casco Bay)

Places that probably belong in a guide but could not be verified with a usable 2025–26 source (or need a decision). One line each.

- **Day's Crabmeat & Lobster**, 1269 US Route 1, Yarmouth — classic seafood shack (built 1920s by the Hiltons, Day family from 1946, sold 2018 per Mainebiz); no 2025–26 source found; its old domain dayscrabmeatandlobster.com is now an unrelated spam site and its Toast page returns 403. Also an "oldest" candidate if the 1920s/1946 history at this address holds.
- **Erica's Seafood**, Basin Point, Harpswell — takeout stand and lobster pound; its own site still shows the 2024 season (last day Oct. 14, 2024); the Harpswell Business Association listing is undated; Harpswell Anchor (Aug. 2026) mentions its dock but not the takeout.
- **Holbrook's Wharf & Grill**, 986 Cundy's Harbor Rd, Harpswell — the wharf was busy in 2026 (Cundy's Harbor Days crate races, Harpswell Anchor Sept. 2026) but the grill's hours are Facebook-only; nothing dated shows the grill serving in 2025–26.
- **Fat Boy Drive-In**, 111 Bath Rd, Brunswick — verified open for 2026 (Press Herald June 22, 2026: opened June 26; founded by the Burton family on Bath Road in 1955; listed for sale). Left out only because no kind fits a carhop drive-in.
- **Dairy Frost**, 107 Bath Rd, Brunswick — Press Herald April 2025 ice cream guide says it first opened in 1955; its own site (dairyfrostmaine.com) answers with a bot check (403); no 2026 source.
- **Cormier's Dog House**, 765 Roosevelt Trail, Windham — Press Herald (May 2026) lists a Maine red snapper among its dogs, but the specialty is Chicago-style Vienna Beef dogs, and its own site returns 403. Red snapper is not its signature.
- **Lonnie Dogs**, Gray — red-snapper-only hot dog cart since 1978 (Press Herald, May 2025); no fixed street address in a usable source.
- **Island Lobster Co.**, 20 Island Ave, Peaks Island (Portland) — lobster restaurant from its own boat; its site lists hours but has no 2025–26 date (only Google review text, which we can't use).
- **Marcy's Diner**, 47 Oak St, Portland — opened 1989 (Portland Food Map); no 2025–26 source found; its old domain nicemuffin.com is now an unrelated casino site.
- **Other Side Diner**, 500 Washington Ave, Portland — sold in late May 2026 (Portland Food Map, June 1, 2026); the new owners planned to relaunch in July 2026; no source yet confirms it reopened.
- **Brunswick Diner**, Brunswick — classic diner; no 2025–26 source found.
- **Bob’s Seafood**, Windham — clam shack; no 2025–26 source found (bobsseafood.net is a New Jersey business).
- **Corsetti's**, Westbrook — Maine Italians, in Westbrook since the 1970s, owned by Eli Small since 2018 (Press Herald, Feb. 2025); no street address in a usable source.
- **Severino's** (Westbrook) and **Quality Shop** (473 Stevens Ave, Portland; at that address since 1970 per Portland Food Map) — named as Italian-sandwich favorites in the Press Herald (Feb. 2025) only via a Facebook group; not verified.
- **The Clambake Seafood Restaurant**, Pine Point Rd, Scarborough — Press Herald (Mar. 2026): reopened for the season March 12 and, with Ken's Place, "in business since 1927" (wording ambiguous); Facebook-only; no street number in a usable source. Likely clam shack.
- **Fat Pat's Take Out**, Harpswell Islands Rd (Route 24), Harpswell — long-closed clam shack; owners planned a 2026 reopening (Press Herald Dec. 2025), but Harpswell Anchor (June 27, 2026) says no Fat Pat's in 2026.
- **Salt Cod Cafe**, 1894 Harpswell Islands Rd, Orr's Island (Harpswell) — seasonal cafe at the Cribstone Bridge (Harpswell Anchor, July 2025); the business association listing mentions lobster rolls, chowder and fruit pies; kind fit is weak.
- **Pammy's Ice Cream Parlor**, 1410 Harpswell Neck Rd (Mitchell Field), Harpswell — Facebook-only.
- **Morse's Food Truck**, 119 Lookout Point Rd, Harpswell — seafood truck, Memorial Day to Columbus Day per an undated business-association listing.
- **The Mosquito** (Raymond, 30th year in 2026), **Causeway Dairy Bar** and **Lakeside Dairy Bar** (Naples Causeway), **Maine Street Creamery** (Bridgton) — confirmed in the Press Herald's July 6, 2026 Route 302 ice cream guide, but no street addresses in a usable source.
- **Caddy's Ice Cream Shack** (169 Gray Rd, Cumberland), **The Dairy Corner** (612 Route 1, Scarborough), **Papa's Ice Cream** (62 Ossipee Trail, Standish), **Super Scoops** (178 Route 1, Falmouth), **Twin Swirls** (865 Brighton Ave, Portland), **Mainely Custard** (150 Route 1, Freeport), **The Maine Scoop** (Pine Point Rd, Scarborough; the guide prints "161 Pint Point Road") — dairy bars in the Press Herald April 2025 ice cream guide, mostly Facebook-only; left out to keep dairy bars from swamping the region; can be added from that source.
- **Sammy's Scoops**, 51 Ocean Ave, Portland — Gifford's and soft-serve stand; its site shows only a 2025 copyright and undated Instagram posts.
- **Town Line Diner**, Route 302, Westbrook — new diner in the former Pine Tree Bakery building (Portland Food Map, Jan. and May 2026); Facebook-only.
- **Chebeague Island Inn**, 61 South Rd, Chebeague Island — historic island inn with a dining room; no dated 2025–26 source or verified founding year.
- **Boone's Fish House & Oyster Room**, 86 Commercial St (Custom House Wharf), Portland — open (Maine Lobster Week 2026) and says "since 1898" with the original baked stuffed lobster, but Portland Food Map lists the original Boone's as 1898–200? (closed, later reopened), so not "oldest"; could go in as "oyster bar" if wanted.
- **Dolphin Marina & Restaurant**, 515 Basin Point Rd, Harpswell — open in 2026 (its site: founded 1966, 60th year, last day of season Oct. 17); known for fish chowder; no kind fits.
- **Island Creek Oyster Shop**, 123 Washington Ave, Portland — Portland Food Map links only Facebook.
- **Maine Oyster Company**, 38 Portland St, Portland — site is current (Maine OysterFest 2026) but unclear whether it runs a raw bar at that address.
- **Ruski's Tavern**, 212 Danforth St, Portland — Press Herald (2026) says it dates to 1892; Portland Food Map says Ruski's opened in 1982. "Oldest" unverified.
- **Ken's Place** (Scarborough, 1927) and **Bayley's Lobster Pound** (Scarborough, 1915) — both in the guide, but not tagged "oldest" because no source confirms they have been at their current addresses since then.
