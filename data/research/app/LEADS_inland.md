# Inland Maine leads (not verified for the guide)

One line each: place, town, address if known, why it isn't in `inland.json`.

## Franco-American
- **Dolly's Restaurant**, Frenchville, 17 US Route 1 — the Valley's best-known ployes/cretons/poutine diner (Down East 2020 and 2023), but no dated 2025–26 source we may use: the UMFK and Visit Aroostook listings are undated, the only 2026 mention is a readers' poll (Best of Aroostook), and its "website" is Facebook. Strongest lead in the region.
- **Lakeview Restaurant**, St. Agatha, 9 Lakeview Dr — open in Jan 2025 as the Long Lake ice-fishing derby base (BDN 2025-01-15), but no usable source ties it to ployes or poutine (only polls); own site lakeviewrestaurant.biz returns HTTP 526.
- **T&D Eatery**, Madawaska (food truck, temporary spot at 82 Mill St) — opened July 3, 2026 with Acadian food (chicken stew with ployes, pot en pot, pâté chinois) per BDN 2026-07-12; left out because the spot is temporary.
- **Two Rivers Lunch**, Allagash, 75 Dickey Rd — poutine, hot-dog-stand roots in the 1970s (Down East 2023); no 2025–26 source.
- **Rails**, Lewiston, 103 Lincoln St — menu built on Franco dishes (poutine, cretons, tourtière) in older Sun Journal/Maine Mag pieces (2017, undated); current status unknown, no site found.
- **Italian Bakery**, Lewiston — one of four Lewiston tourtière makers in the Sun Journal's 2015 tasting; status and address unverified.
- **E.W. Mailhot Sausage Co.**, Lewiston, 258 Bartlett St — Franco-American cretons, tourtière and blood sausage since 1910 (own site), but its site is undated and it reads as a producer selling through stores, with no confirmed retail counter.
- **Pinky D's Poutine Factory** — poutine food truck (Sun Journal 2024); no fixed address.
- **Inn of Acadia restaurant**, Madawaska, 384 St. Thomas St — a Down East food list (seen only as a search snippet, not read) mentions made-to-order breakfast ployes; its current site lists dinner only (Tue–Sat) and says nothing about ployes.

## Diners, bakeries, whoopie pies
- **A1 Diner**, Gardiner, 3 Bridge St — 1946 Worcester diner car; newest usable source is Down East July 2023; a1diner.com is a parked domain; the 2025 Down East Best of Maine mention is a readers' poll.
- **Appalachian Trail Cafe**, Millinocket, 210 Penobscot Ave — long-running diner (whopper flapjacks, squash doughnut; Down East 2023); no 2025–26 source; atcafe.com blocks ClaudeBot in robots.txt.
- **Governor's Restaurant & Bakery**, Old Town (original of the 1959 chain) — won two whoopie pie prizes at the June 2026 Maine Whoopie Pie Festival (Piscataquis Observer 2026-06-15) and its site lists Old Town hours, but no street address is published on the site.
- **Elaine's Basket Cafe**, Milo — whoopie pie vendor/prize winner at the 2025 and 2026 festivals (Piscataquis Observer); no address or own site found.
- **Katherine's Bakery and Cafe**, Norway — whoopie pie prizes 2025 and 2026 (Piscataquis Observer); no address or own site found.
- **Valley View Orchard Pies**, Oxford — whoopie pie prizes 2025 and 2026; possible blueberry-pie lead; no address found.
- **Through the Tulips Bakery** and **BC Sweets** (Milo), **Baked by Joy** (Hermon), **Backyard Bakery** (Dixmont), **Shady Tree Farm** (Livermore Falls) — 2026 whoopie pie festival bakers (Piscataquis Observer 2026-06-15); no addresses.
- **Frank's Bake Shop**, Bangor — classic Bangor bakery; franksbakeshop.com answers with a bot check, so not fetched.
- **Bolley's Famous Franks**, Hallowell, 38 Water St — hot dog stand since 1958 (Press Herald 2026-05-04), open Tue–Sat; natural-casing franks, not red snappers, and 1958 is too late for "oldest", so no kind fits.
- **Village Inn and Tavern**, Belgrade Lakes, 157 Main St — opened about 1921 as the Locust House (Down East 2023), so a possible "oldest"; no 2025–26 source; villageinnmaine.com is parked.
- **The Birches Resort**, Rockwood, 281 Birches Rd — dining room open (site notice dated August 2026), about 90 years old (Down East 2023); left out because Rockwood is in unorganized territory (municipality name unsure) and the year meals began there isn't stated.
- **Sam's Italian Foods' original shop**, Lewiston, Main St — the chain's own history still calls 286 Main St its "present location," but Main St downtown is missing from its 2026 locations page; a NEWS CENTER Maine story says the Lewiston shop closed, but that site returned 403 to us, so this is unconfirmed.

## Dairy bars
- **Houlton Farms Dairy bars**, Houlton, Presque Isle, Caribou — seasonal bars since 1983; BDN (2024) says they would stay open after milk production stopped, but no 2025–26 opening and no addresses found; houltonfarms.com unreachable.
- **Al's Dairy Freeze**, Fort Kent, 16 Hall St — on UMFK's current (undated) dining list only.
- More stands in the Press Herald 2025 ice cream guide that we didn't add: Big Dipper (Lisbon Falls), Fielder's Choice (Sabattus, Manchester), Freeze Kompany (Mechanic Falls), The Inside Scoop and Norway/Paris Soft Serve (South Paris), Jimmies (Mexico), Sugar Shack (Bethel), Ice Cream Shoppe (Oakland, Randolph, Skowhegan). Sun Journal 2026: Wilson Stream Ice Cream and Family Freeze (Wilton), The Farmers Daughter (Oquossoc).
